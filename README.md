# GrooveCrate

My Discogs collection ([Groovecrate](https://www.discogs.com/user/Groovecrate/collection)), Netflix style.

- **Browse** 370 records in rows by style, genre and decade, plus the wantlist, the most valuable records and the hard-to-find ones
- **Search** by artist, title, label or catalogue number
- **Marketplace**: every Discogs copy for sale, sorted by total price delivered to Ireland, with filters for condition, seller country (Ireland / EU / anywhere) and a price cap
- **Crate Digger**: a 3D record crate you flick through like in a shop (scroll, swipe or use the arrow keys), with bins by style, A–Z jumps, price stickers and a listening-post auto-play mode
- **Player**: play a record's tracks (YouTube clips linked on Discogs) from a bar that keeps playing while you browse, with a queue and tracklists
- **Indie shop finder**: one-click stock searches at Irish, UK and European independent record shops (Spindizzy, Freebird, All City, Tower, Phonica, Sounds of the Universe, Juno, Rough Trade, Rush Hour, Hard Wax, HHV and more), plus eBay, Bandcamp, Google Shopping and Popsike

It's one self-contained `index.html` with no build step. Cover art loads from the Discogs image CDN.

Data is a snapshot taken from the Discogs API and marketplace on 15 Nov 2026. Prices are in EUR and listings may have sold since.

Not affiliated with Discogs or Netflix.
